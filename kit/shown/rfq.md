# Request for Quotation RFQ-FLV-2026-01: Fluvoro gutter-outlet leaf shredder

**From:** Kestrel Home Systems GmbH, Product Management (fictional customer, synthetic project)
**Date:** 2 November 2026
**Quotation due:** 30 November 2026
**Questions until:** 20 November 2026 (in writing; answers are shared with all bidders)

## 1. Subject

Kestrel Home Systems plans to launch **Fluvoro**, a battery-powered, solar-charged
leaf shredder that sits in the gutter outlet and keeps it from clogging. We ask you
to quote the **system and software development** of Fluvoro, from requirements
through qualified software, including the functional-safety work.

The attached **Customer Requirements Specification (CRS) v1**,
`fluvoro_crs_v1.reqif`, with 101 requirements, is the binding technical basis of
this request.

## 2. Scope of the quotation

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Variants | **V1 insert** (retrofit, homeowner-installed) and **V2 replacement outlet** (roofer-installed), first size NW 333 / DN 87–100 |
| Option | **Connectivity option** (Bluetooth Low Energy app), quoted as a separate item |
| System engineering | SYS.1–SYS.5 to Automotive SPICE 4.0: requirements analysis, system architecture, integration and qualification |
| Software engineering | SWE.1–SWE.6: software requirements, architecture, detailed design and implementation (C), unit verification, integration and qualification |
| Functional safety | Development to ISO 26262:2018, tailored to the item (CRS chapter 6): item definition, HARA with ASIL determination, functional and technical safety concepts, safety case |
| Project and support | MAN.3, SUP.1, SUP.8, SUP.9, SUP.10 |

**Not in scope:** industrial design, tooling and series production, the smartphone
app itself (only the device side of the BLE interface), and certification fees.
Hardware and mechanical design appear only as far as the system architecture and
the hardware–software interface need them.

## 3. What we ask you to quote

1. **Effort per work package**, in person-days by role, for the scope above, with
   the connectivity option shown separately.
2. **Project plan**, with the milestones B-sample software (feature complete) and
   C-sample software (qualified, safety case complete).
3. **Your assumptions and the open points** you need us to clarify.
4. **Deliverables list**, cross-checked against CRS FLV-STK-142.

## 4. Schedule we are aiming for

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Kick-off | February 2027 |
| B-sample software | October 2027 |
| C-sample software | April 2028 |
| Start of production | September 2028 (before the autumn season) |

## 5. Points still open on our side

Please state your assumption for each. We will answer questions in writing.

1. **Volume split** between V1 and V2 in the first two years.
2. **Whether the connectivity option** will be offered from start of production or added later.
3. **Hardware partner:** whether you also propose the electronics hardware design, or
   only the system and software with a hardware partner we name.
